Why audivize

Communication shouldn't require everyone to learn sign language

Current transcription apps break down in group settings. Passing a phone around just isn't a real solution. Audivize pairs every spoken word to a face — so you always know who said what.

How it works

Lightweight AI, powerful results — all on your iPhone.

The process

From sound to screen in real time

1

Microphone picks up speech

The app listens using your iPhone's mic — no external hardware needed.

2

On-device AI processes audio

A lightweight model transcribes speech and identifies each unique speaker locally — no internet required.

3

Camera matches voice to face

Computer vision pairs each speaker's voice to their face in the camera view.

4

Words appear on screen

Each word is shown with the speaker's name — you always know who said what.

Technology

Built with accessibility in mind

Speech processing

Lightweight speech-to-text optimized for real-time mobile use.

Speaker diarization

Separates and identifies multiple speakers in one audio stream.

Face linking

Ties each speaker's voice ID to their face on camera.

About Audivize

A student-built project with a real-world mission.

Our mission

Empowering people everywhere to join the conversation

Audivize was built around one question: why should your ability to communicate depend on others learning sign language? We are building on-device AI that makes group conversations accessible to people with hearing loss — no special hardware, no privacy tradeoffs, no barriers.
